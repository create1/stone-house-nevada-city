Common questions before booking

How far in advance do couples typically book?

Most wedding dates are reserved 12–18 months out, with peak May–October weekends booking earliest. Off-season dates (November through April) and weekday celebrations often have more flexibility.

Can we tour the venue before requesting pricing?

Yes — we host periodic open house events and offer private tours by appointment. The pamphlet you'll receive after submitting the form above includes the next available tour windows.

Is the venue available for elopements or smaller celebrations?

Absolutely. Our elopement and micro-wedding offerings use the smaller spaces (The Parlor, The Penthouse) for intimate ceremonies of 2–30 guests, with separate pricing from full weddings.
