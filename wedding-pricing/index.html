A Historic California Event Venue

Where history, sustainability & culinary excellence meet

Within a beautifully restored 1857 building, Stone House blends timeless charm with a modern event venue and elevated hospitality. Our commitment to nourishing the community goes beyond the venue.

Our catering is crafted with the highest-quality ingredients and is always seed-oil free. Whether it’s a wedding, a birthday, or any celebration, Stone House is where memorable events come to life.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What secures our date?
A 50% deposit reserves your date.
What’s the rain plan for an outdoor ceremony?
If it rains, we move the ceremony indoors to the Showroom — your built-in backup.
Are you pet-friendly?
Yes, we’re pet-friendly.
How close are hotels & nightlife?
We’re one block from The National Exchange Hotel, in the heart of downtown Nevada City.
How long is our day?
Your full-building rental is a 12-hour block, plus 2–3 hours beforehand to get ready in the suite.
Do we need a wedding planner?
Yes — a planner is required. You’re welcome to bring your own or hire one of our coordinators.
