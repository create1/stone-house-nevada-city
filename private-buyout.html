Private Buyout Venue

When you book Stone House, you book the building — the clearest differentiator in Nevada County wedding venues.

The whole building is yours

Stone House does not share your celebration with another party. A private buyout means ceremony, cocktail hour, dinner, and dancing stay under one historic roof — Great Hall, Courtyard, Showroom, Cavern, get-ready suites, and more.

That model is rare in Gold Country. Most venues sell a room or a lawn while another event runs next door. Here, your guest list gets the full property.

Why couples choose a buyout

  • No competing music, parking, or guest traffic
  • Seven spaces for ceremony, cocktails, dinner, and photos
  • In-house seed-oil-free catering included in the experience
  • Capacity up to 300 with flexible layouts
