Private corporate venue in Gold Country

Stone House is a full private buyout venue in downtown Nevada City. When you book, the building is yours — seven spaces, in-house seed-oil-free catering, and no competing events under the same roof.

Teams use Stone House for corporate retreats, board dinners, product launches, holiday parties, and client entertainment. Sacramento is about an hour away; the Bay Area is a straightforward drive for overnight offsites.

What companies book

  • Leadership and board dinners in the Great Hall or Penthouse
  • Day retreats with breakout rooms across connected spaces
  • Company holiday parties and year-end celebrations
  • Client entertainment and partner summits
