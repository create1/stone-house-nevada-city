What's included in our event pricing

Every Stone House event price covers the venue itself — exclusive access to the spaces you've booked, on-site staffing, and the operational coordination that comes with a full-service historic venue. Beyond venue use, our pricing pamphlets break out: in-house chef-prepared menu options across three tiers, beverage and bar service, ceremony fees (where applicable), space rental for each of our seven distinct rooms, and any add-ons specific to your event type.

Pricing varies meaningfully across event types — a corporate offsite for 40 has different requirements than a 150-guest wedding reception. Selecting your event type in the form above routes you to the relevant pricing pamphlet so you're not sorting through information that doesn't apply to your celebration.

Common questions

How does Stone House handle pricing transparency?

All pricing is listed in our pamphlets — no hidden fees, no per-guest extras for standard service, and no minimum-spend surprises after your tour. Couples receive the full breakdown before signing anything.

Are there separate pricing structures for weddings, private events, and corporate?

Yes. Each event type has its own pamphlet because the package structure differs. Wedding pricing is built around ceremony + reception packages. Private events (birthdays, anniversaries, milestone gatherings) are priced by space and guest count. Corporate events include offsite-specific pricing for half-day, full-day, and multi-day buyouts.

Can we see real photos and previous events before requesting pricing?

Yes — browse real wedding gallery and our blog for behind-the-scenes coverage, menu philosophy, and guest experience details.
