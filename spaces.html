Seven Distinctive Rooms

Every space tells
a different story

Space 01 of 07

The Great Hall

Up to 130 cocktails · 100 seated dinner

Soaring ceilings. Original stone.

The heart of Stone House — ceremony, reception, or the long night under timber and stone when the whole building is yours.

Space 02 of 07

The Cavern

Up to 30 seated

Ancient arches. Candlelit warmth.

An intimate stone chamber for vows, first looks, or a private dinner that feels discovered rather than designed.

Space 03 of 07

The Penthouse

Private lodging

City views. Stay on-site.

A private suite above the celebration — get ready here, return after last dance, and never leave the building you just made yours.

Space 04 of 07

The Courtyard

Ceremony 150 · Dinner 100

Open sky. Stone walls. Golden light.

Outdoor ceremony and al fresco celebration framed by the building’s original walls — then straight inside when the evening cools.

Space 05 of 07

The Showroom

Up to 90 seated · 2nd floor

Stone walls. Timber beams. Elegant.

A refined second-floor hall for ceremonies, seated dinners, or presentations that need presence without leaving downtown Nevada City — equipped with a Meyer line array sound system and GrandMA lighting control.

Space 06 of 07

The Parlor

Up to 100 cocktails · 300 combined

Two bars. First toast to last dance.

Cocktail energy with historic soul — where guests gather between moments and stay for the night’s best conversations.

Space 07 of 07

The Lounge

Up to 50 seated · 150 combined · 2nd floor

Craft cocktails. Plush seating. Elegant.

A softer upstairs room for intimate dinners, lounging service, or a second reception room when the Great Hall is full of dancing.
