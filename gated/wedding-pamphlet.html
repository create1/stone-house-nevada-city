A Historic California Event Venue

Within a beautifully restored 1857 historic building, Stone House blends timeless charm with a modern event venue and elevated hospitality experiences. Our commitment to nourishing the community goes beyond the venue.

Our catering is crafted with the highest quality ingredients and always seed oil free. Whether you’re here for a wedding, a birthday or any kind of event, Stone House offers a space where history, sustainability, and culinary excellence come together.

The Spaces

7 spaces.

Parlour

A mesmerizing bar and exquisite cocktails await you in a casual and riveting space downstairs.

Cocktail service for up to 100 or connect to the Showroom and host up to 200 guests.

The Lounge

Offering the finest craft cocktails, plush leather seating, and gorgeous wood tables. A majestic room that will leave your guests in awe.

Offering table service for up to 50 guests, or combine it with the adjacent Great Hall to accommodate table service for up to 150 people.

The Great Hall

Stone walls, timber beams and elegant lighting set the backdrop for your group’s formal dining experience.

Seats up to 100 guests at our rustic wooden tables.

The Courtyard

Perfect for an outdoor wedding ceremony and al fresco dining. Framed by romantic stone walls, an intimate wine cavern, and a picturesque brick stairway, this space nestled against the backdrop of Nevada City’s nature is the ideal setting for a Gold Country event you’ll never forget.

Seating up to 150 for a ceremony and 100 for seated dinner service.

The Cavern

Built in the late 1800’s as a brewery, you can make it your wedding hideaway, invite your guests in for wine and whiskey tastings, or host a small dinner.

Fits up to 30 guests.

The Showroom

Featuring a state-of-the-art sound and lighting system and wooden dance floor, there is no better place in the Sierra Foothills to dance the night away.

Invite up to 300 guests for cocktails and dancing. Or we’ll set up a dinner for 50 stage-side.

The Suite

Our 3 bedroom penthouse suite is a perfect place to accommodate your daytime relaxation and overnight stay. Equipped with a steam shower, copper tub and kitchen for your every need.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is required to secure your date?
We require a 50% deposit in order to secure your date.
If it’s an outdoor ceremony, what’s the plan B if it rains?
If it rains, we will move the ceremony to the showroom as the backup indoor location.
Are you pet-friendly?
Yes, we are pet-friendly.
How close are you to hotels and local entertainment?
We are one block from The National Exchange Hotel.
What is the timing of a typical wedding day?
A typical wedding day runs from 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
Do I need to hire a wedding planner?
Yes, a wedding planner is required.
