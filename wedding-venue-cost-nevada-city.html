Wedding Venue Cost Nevada City

Transparent starting points for a private buyout wedding at Stone House — historic downtown venue with catering included.

What couples budget for

Stone House wedding costs center on a private venue buyout plus in-house catering and beverage packages. Published starting points begin around $6,500 for venue packages, with peak Saturday buyouts higher — see current figures on our pricing page and the wedding pricing pamphlet.

What is usually included

  • Exclusive use of the 1857 building and event spaces
  • Tables, china, stemware, and silverware for large guest counts
  • Seed-oil-free catering tiers (buffet or plated)
  • Optional open-bar packages and ceremony setups

Costs vary by season, day of week, guest count, and menu tier. For a written quote, contact bookings or download the pamphlet after sharing your details.
